How it all began

From best friends to forever...

13 February 2016

Where it all began

It all started in Valletta. Alexia had a feeling there was something special about Nathan, and what began as a friendship quickly became something neither of them could imagine living without. We shared everything to laughing over the smallest things, we became each other's favourite person… even if Nathan took a little longer to catch on!

4 January 2017

Finally, a first date!

New Year's Eve came and went without the midnight kiss Alexia hoped for! But just a few days later, on 4 January, Nathan finally decided to give them a chance. And just like that our friendship turned into a love story… the rest is history! Nearly ten years of love, laughter, growing together, and countless beautiful memories later, here we are — still choosing each other, every single day.

11 October 2023

We bought a home!

Sharing everything wasn't enough… so we decided to share a home loan too! We found a place to call our own, ready to turn it into a home filled with love, laughter, and lots of memories.

22 August 2025

Home is wherever we are

Moving in together marked the beginning of another exciting chapter. We found own little world — a place of comfort, peace, endless love, where the simplest moments became our favourite ones. Because, at the end of the day, home was never just a place, it was wherever we were together.

9 April 2026

The “Yes” day!

The plan was Dubai. The reality? A romantic getaway to Matera! Sometimes the best moments happen when life has other plans. Nathan popped the question in the most beautiful setting, and Alexia said YES! (Thankfully, this plan worked out perfectly)

3 April 2027

To our next chapter

From a group of friends and strangers crossing paths in Valletta to a lifetime of adventures ahead, our story has brought us to this very moment. Back in Valletta, we are now ready to get married, and we can't imagine celebrating it without all our favourite people by our side.
